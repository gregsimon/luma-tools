How the Librarian works:

The Librarian connects to your Google Drive to store and retrieve your Luma-1 sounds securely.

  • Open: Use the Google Picker to select audio files or banks from anywhere in your Drive.
  • Save: Save your work to any folder you choose.
  • Secure:Note this app only has access to the specific files you select or create.
